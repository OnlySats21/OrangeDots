STRC vs Par — Why It Matters

Above $100: Strategy can issue new shares through ATM. Every dollar raised buys Bitcoin. Gate OPEN.

$99.50–$100: Reduced issuance. Strategy may still sell but at a discount — limits capital raised. Gate REDUCED.

Below $99.50: ATM effectively closed. No meaningful new shares issued. Bitcoin purchases pause or rely on MSTR.

Ex-dividend effect: STRC mechanically drops below $100 each month on the ex-date (Apr 15), then recovers as dividend is set. Expected above par again ~May 6-7.
